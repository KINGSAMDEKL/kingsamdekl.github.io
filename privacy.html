Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

At KINGSAMTECH PRO, accessible from our GitHub Pages site, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected and how it is utilized.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse KINGSAMTECH PRO, you can enjoy all articles, guides, and the Web Sandbox without providing personal identification. If you opt to subscribe to our newsletter or contact us, we collect only your provided email address solely to deliver requested updates.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

KINGSAMTECH PRO uses browser localStorage solely to save your local preferences: your choice between Dark and Light mode, and your "Read Later" saved bookmarks. This data remains on your local machine and is never transmitted to external servers.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookies & Advertising

Google is a third-party advertising partner on our site. Google uses cookies, including DART cookies, to serve ads to our visitors based on their visit to our site and other destinations across the web. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

4. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

We respect your privacy rights under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You have the right to request access, rectification, or erasure of any personal data you have voluntarily provided.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, contact us at: kingsamtech@gmail.com.
